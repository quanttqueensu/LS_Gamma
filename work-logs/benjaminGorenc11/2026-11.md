## 2026-11-17 19:08 · Deep_Hedging
**Update (by Ben, drafted with Claude):**
- Built a walk-forward deep hedger for the live put20 book: a small neural net picks how much of the put's delta to hedge each morning (0–150%), trained on real R2 trades with the book's stops, profit-takes and costs, and retrained each January on past data only.
- Added a research-only `deep_hedge` switch to the backtest engine (off by default; put20 still reproduces Gabe's $149,893 exactly), pre-registered the card, and ran 6 trials on the development window. The hold-out was not touched.
- Result: failed 8 of 14 checks. P&L was higher ($172k vs $150k at 1x), but Sharpe fell to 0.52 from 0.78 and drawdown hit $96k, over the $87.5k budget. All of the extra P&L came from March–April 2020; without that window it made $11k less than put20.
- Learned: the net became a dip-buyer instead of a hedger (it hedged ~129% of delta when VIX was under 13 and ~59% above 35), and the best fixed hedge share came out at 100% of delta. Today's full delta hedge is hard to beat, and a profit-seeking objective invites speculation.
- Next: try a risk-only objective with the hedge kept within 80–120% of delta, and train on simulated crash paths before testing on R2. Watch out: `lsgamma.hub.export` overwrites registry.csv with local runs only.
